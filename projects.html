Meridian Frontier — Live Trade, Environment & Development Dashboard

Designed, built and maintain a live economics research dashboard that pulls World Bank data in real time, client-side, across 37 indicators and 200+ economies. Focused narrowly on the intersection of trade, environment, agriculture and development, it goes beyond a data portal with four analytical tools: an indicator explorer with CSV export, a relationship plotter (OLS fit with Pearson correlation), an event study built around policy years such as EUDR, CBAM and Paris (difference-in-differences), and a structural-break finder that detects change-points via a Chow-test sup-F sweep. Every figure is fetched live from the source and the relationship views are explicitly labelled as descriptive, not causal.
